WIREFRAMING 02 · iVEGAN

Designing clear journeys on mobile

Adapting the iVegan redesign for smartphones: content priorities, touch controls, compact navigation and a wireflow that preserves shopping continuity.

The screens shown here use images of the original wireframes. You can also explore the Figma project for the full detail.

01 · MOBILE WIREFRAME SYSTEM

Adapt the structure, do not shrink desktop

Pt. 2 retains the Minor Third scale (1.2 ratio) and Inter font, adapting headings to the mobile context. The headline values shown in the slides are 40, 32, 26, 22 and 18px, with 16px body text.

The mobile grid consists of 4 columns, with 16px gutters and margins ; controls prioritise recognisable touch areas, primary buttons are 56pt high and icons have a tappable area of at least 44px. In the wireframe, blue and greys identify priorities and interactions.

02 · MOBILE WIREFRAMES

Six screens, one consistent hierarchy

The mobile work covers Home, Product Listing Page, Product Details Page, Cart, Account and the Ethics & Sustainability Hub. Each layout reinterprets the desktop content according to priorities, available space and touch interaction patterns.

01 · HOME

Discovery and priority actions

Top bar with language and app; hamburger, account and cart visible; offers and reviews explored with horizontal scrolling.

02 · PLP

Exploration without overload

Simplified back breadcrumb, circular types, filters and sorting in panels, vertically stacked products and “Load more” every 10 items.

03 · PDP

Detail through progressive disclosure

Swipeable gallery, explicit availability, linked brand, detail accordion and horizontally scrolling related products.

04 · CART

Control and transparency

Closable pop-up with total, thresholds, discounts and shipping; full cart for quantities and suggestions. The voucher is entered in the pop-up, not on the cart page.

05 · ACCOUNT

Understandable forms

Separate personal, delivery and billing details, persistent labels and address autocomplete.

06 · ETHICS HUB

Trust that leads to the shop

Four vertical areas — ethics, sustainability, supply chain and certifications — with further details and a final link to products.

03 · MOBILE WIREFLOW

From the hamburger menu to checkout

The mobile wireflow documents the path between the screens that were actually designed: menu, Vegan meat category, PLP, PDP and add to cart. The cart opens as a pop-up, from which users can continue to checkout or view the full cart.

The presentation clarifies that the complete checkout screen is documented in the desktop wireframes ; it is therefore not presented as a mobile screen designed in this phase.

  4. 04 Cart pop-up

04 · DESIGN DIRECTION

Consistency between navigation and conversion

Component decisions are not isolated: expandable filters reduce visual noise, scrollable cards support discovery, accordion details aid reading, and the cart pop-up concentrates decisive information before checkout.

The result is a medium-fidelity mobile wireframing proposal, not a developed version or one validated through usability testing.

IDENTIFIED PAIN POINT PROPOSAL
Limited screen space

Simultaneous controls and content increase visual load.

→
Progressive disclosure

Filters, accordions and menus open when needed.

Discontinuous shopping journey

Screen changes and cost information are fragmented.

→
Persistent cart

Overlay with total, thresholds and clearly distinguished actions.

Access to brand values

Trust content separated from commercial navigation.

→
Hub connected to the shop

Vertical thematic cards with detail CTAs.
