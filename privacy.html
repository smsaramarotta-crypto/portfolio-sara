PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sara Marotta processes personal data submitted through the contact form on this website.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Sara Marotta. For any request concerning personal data, you can use the contact form and enter “Privacy request” as the subject.

2. Data collected

The contact form may collect:

  • first and last name;
  • pronouns, if provided voluntarily;
  • email address;
  • request subject;
  • message content.

Fields marked as required are necessary to submit and manage the request. Optional information may be omitted.

3. Purposes of processing

Data is processed solely to receive, manage and respond to the request submitted through the contact form and, where necessary, to follow up on the same request.

Data is not used for marketing, profiling or promotional communications.

4. Tools used

Data submitted through the form is transmitted via Google Apps Script and recorded in a Google Sheets spreadsheet used to manage requests.

The spreadsheet is connected to Zapier to automate notifications. When a new row is created, Zapier sends me an email notification about the request received.

5. Parties that may process the data

Data may be processed by Sara Marotta and by the technical providers used to operate the form and automations, including Google and Zapier, solely for the purposes described in this policy.

The Google Sheets spreadsheet is not public and access is limited to the data controller.

6. Retention period

Data is retained in the Google Sheets spreadsheet, email notifications and related activity for a maximum of 30 days after the last communication relating to the request.

At the end of this period, the data is deleted from the tools used to manage the request, unless required by law or necessary to protect a legal right.

7. Data subject rights

You may request access to your data, its rectification or deletion, restriction of processing, or object to processing where provided for by applicable law.

To exercise your rights, you can use the contact form and enter “Privacy request” as the subject.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority .

8. Updates

This policy may be updated over time to reflect changes to the website, form or tools used.
