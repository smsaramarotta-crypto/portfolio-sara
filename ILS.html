Redesigning the digital experience and streamlining processes

A multidisciplinary project for an Italian language school in Sydney, combining UX/UI design, front-end, automations, analytics and marketing.

PROJECT

A clearer digital ecosystem for students and the school

Italian Language Studio is an adult Italian language school based in Sydney. The project began from the need to make the website clearer, more recognisable and more focused on enrolment.

The work covered the redesign of the school’s digital experience, from site structure and enrolment flows to automations, tracking and communication.

The project was managed directly across its different stages, combining UX/UI design, web design, front-end customisation, brand direction, automations, analytics and performance marketing.

PROBLEM

A fragmented experience

The previous website did not guide users effectively: it was not immediately clear which courses were available, how to choose the right level, where to find the calendar and resources, or what the next step towards enrolment should be.

The brand identity was also underdeveloped, and some processes required manual steps or workarounds because of platform limitations.

The risk was creating confusion for both students and the school’s internal operations.

OBJECTIVES

Making the journey simpler

  • clarify the course offering;
  • simplify navigation and enrolment;
  • improve the responsive experience;
  • strengthen the brand identity;
  • automate selected internal processes;
  • connect the website to tracking tools and marketing campaigns;
  • use data to continuously improve UX and content.

UX & INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Course navigation and structure

Reducing search and making journeys more direct

The website navigation was reorganised so users could start from their Italian level or from the type of course they wanted. Level pages provide quick access to the available standard, conversation and combined options without requiring users to search across multiple pages.

Alternative paths were also created from the main courses section, with clearer explanations of the different course types and direct links to the course list, calendar and free resources.

The new structure also addressed issues identified through the user experience: content such as the calendar and free materials was difficult to find, so it was made more visible in the navigation and page layouts.

The cards were redesigned to be more recognisable as interactive elements through shadows, hover states, stronger contrast and clearer visual hierarchy. Parts of the layout were also customised with HTML and CSS to improve readability, spacing and accessibility.

02

USER FLOWS & ENROLMENT EXPERIENCE

Course enrolment flows

Designing different paths for different course types

The school’s courses used different enrolment methods. Separate flows were therefore designed for group courses, combined courses and private lessons, adapting each step to the needs of the specific journey.

For standard and conversation courses, the flow was simplified so users could quickly reach the course list, either from the level menu or from the main course-offering page.

For combined courses, LearnWorlds limitations made it impossible to create a flexible bundle with courses selected by the user. A workaround based on a custom form, payment, Google Sheets and Zapier was designed to automate enrolment in the selected courses and reduce the school’s manual work.

For private lessons, the journey starts with an initial request to collect information about the student’s needs, level and availability, followed by a follow-up from the school.

The microcopy was also adapted to the context. For private lessons, for example, the “Get your lesson plan” CTA makes clear that the first step is a personalised request rather than immediate enrolment.

VISUAL DESIGN & BRAND DIRECTION

Brand direction and visual identity

Giving the brand personality

A central part of the redesign was developing a stronger visual direction for Italian Language Studio. The previous website lacked distinction and did not fully communicate the character of the school.

Colours, imagery, visual rhythm, custom components and graphic details were reconsidered to create an identity that felt warmer, more professional and more memorable.

The palette uses natural tones closer to the Italian visual landscape: soft greens, warm tones and earthy accents. The aim was to avoid an overly cold or generic result while maintaining a reliable and professional impression.

Several interface elements were customised with HTML and CSS, adapting components and visual details to the platform’s limitations to create greater consistency with the new brand identity.

More narrative and cultural elements were also introduced, including Italian “fun facts” placed inside a CSS-created graphic shape designed to evoke study, notes and creativity.

The new visual direction balances professionalism, warmth and Italian authenticity through imagery, colours and cultural content.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN & FRONT-END CUSTOMISATION

Responsive design

Optimising the experience across key breakpoints

LearnWorlds manages responsive design through dedicated desktop, tablet and mobile views. The main pages were therefore optimised at the platform’s breakpoints, refining spacing, visual hierarchy, readability and component behaviour.

The work went beyond resizing desktop elements and required a review of information priorities. On tablet and mobile, some sections were simplified by reducing or removing secondary elements that would have made the content harder to scan.

The aim was to keep the key information — courses, levels, calls to action and enrolment steps — immediately visible, making the journey more direct and easier to scan.

Where the platform’s native settings were not sufficient, custom HTML and CSS were used to solve specific builder limitations, particularly in the hamburger menu, submenus and selected footer elements.

On mobile and portrait tablet, the navigation was reorganised by improving alignment, submenu hierarchy, colours and item distribution. The footer was also adapted to different views to make the content more orderly and the main links more accessible.

AUTOMATIONS & SYSTEM DESIGN

Automations

Connecting data collection, automations and operations

To support the school’s internal operations, custom automations were designed and implemented using HTML, CSS, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script and Zapier.

Custom forms and mini quizzes were created for combined courses and private lessons, integrated into the platform to overcome some of LearnWorlds’ native limitations.

Collected data is automatically sent to Google Sheets through Google Apps Script, which handles receiving, validating and organising information in real time.

The spreadsheets are then connected to Zapier to automate enrolments, internal notifications, follow-ups and administrative tasks.

An alternative solution was also designed to manage course deadlines and availability. When a course reaches its maximum number of students or approaches its deadline, the system generates a notification and an operational task.

The automations connect data collection, payments, notifications, deadline monitoring and follow-up in one operational flow.

STUDENT EXPERIENCE & INTERFACE DESIGN

Student area and course list

Making course management more structured

The work also covered the “My Courses” page and the course list visible to students.

LearnWorlds’ default structure did not fully meet the school’s needs: courses were not organised clearly enough, categories were difficult to read and the layout made it harder to find the desired content quickly.

Custom HTML and CSS were used to make the page more structured, improve card readability and make searching easier through more recognisable categories and filters.

Particular attention was given to the mobile experience by increasing the interactive areas of cards, filters and buttons in line with accessibility best practices for touch targets. This made the navigation easier to use on touch devices.

ANALYTICS & PERFORMANCE MARKETING

Tracking and marketing

Connecting UX, campaigns and business goals

The project included setting up and integrating Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads, building a measurement system connected to the school’s business goals.

Two distinct Google Ads campaigns were structured. The Brand campaign was designed to cover searches related to Italian Language Studio and reach users already interested in the school. The Generic campaign was organised to reach new users searching for Italian courses in Sydney, distinguishing standard-course intent from conversation-course intent.

Search terms, keywords and ad performance were monitored regularly. Keyword Planner and historical campaign data were used to focus standard courses on the most relevant local searches, including Sydney, CBD and “near me”.

The tracking system distinguishes primary conversions from events used to analyse user behaviour. Purchases, completed form submissions and qualified calls are treated as conversions, while events such as begin checkout, view level and 75% scroll support funnel analysis.

Google Tag Manager was also used to configure tracking for email and phone clicks, Course Finder Quiz completion and the resulting outcome.

In parallel, the school’s Google Business Profile was created and configured, with information, imagery and local presence managed to improve visibility in location-based searches.

08

DATA-DRIVEN UX & LANDING PAGE DESIGN

Landing page and data-driven redesign

Turning data into design decisions

To support the Google Ads campaigns, a dedicated landing page was designed to bring standard courses, conversation courses, private lessons, combined courses and workshops into a more direct journey. Users can compare the available options without getting lost across different pages.

A two-question mini quiz guides the choice based on Italian level and learning goals. Responses are sent to the school, enabling more targeted follow-up and turning initial uncertainty into a more informed enquiry.

The combined analysis of Google Analytics 4, Google Ads and quiz responses revealed significant interest in Beginner courses and in learning Italian for travelling and communicating in Italy. These insights led to the addition of a Beginner CTA immediately after the Standard Courses hero and the development of the Italian for Travellers course concept.

Tests were also planned around adding dates and presenting future editions, with the aim of measuring demand before scheduling. Data therefore becomes a design tool for defining priorities, content and calls to action.

CONTENT DESIGN & COMMUNICATION STRATEGY

Email, social and editorial strategy

A coherent editorial system beyond the website

To maintain coherent communication beyond the website, the new visual identity was applied to Italian Language Studio newsletters, automated emails and social content.

A modular, responsive monthly template was designed and implemented in Brevo to combine course updates, study tips, Italian culture content and calls to action.

Recipients are segmented using LearnWorlds data and active opt-ins. UTM tracking connects email campaigns to Google Analytics 4, making it possible to analyse traffic, on-site behaviour and conversions.

The editorial plan coordinates email and social content while maintaining a recognisable identity. On social media, professionalism and an informal tone come together through content about everyday Italian life and popular culture, creating a more human and distinctive position than the institutional communication common in the sector.

CONCLUSION

A more coherent digital system

The project evolved from a website redesign into a broader intervention across the school’s digital ecosystem.

UX, interface design, automations, analytics and marketing were integrated into one system, with the aim of making the experience simpler for students and operations more efficient for the school.

The result is a clearer, more recognisable and measurable digital presence that is easier to develop over time.
