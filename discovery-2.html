DISCOVERY 02 · PERSONAS, JOURNEYS & SITEMAP

From insights to clearer journeys

The second part of Discovery explores people’s needs and how their shopping journey unfolds on iVegan.

Two personas, their user journeys and a proposed information architecture emerged from the as-is analysis and survey findings. The aim is to connect every redesign decision to the pain points observed.

01 · PERSONAS

Two perspectives, different needs

The research findings informed two personas: Sonia, a vegan who cares about product origin and quality, and Luca, a vegetarian interested in responsible consumption and the practicality of online shopping.

They do not represent two interviewed people, but design profiles created to explore motivations, behaviours and possible difficulties in the shopping experience.

PERSONA 01 · 33 YEARS OLD

Sonia

Veterinarian · vegan

She is organising a vegan dinner and looks for quality ingredients, including gluten-free and sugar-free options, preferably Made in Italy.

What she looks for
Variety, a transparent supply chain and products aligned with her values.
What holds her back
Unclear categories, hard-to-find reviews and confusing discount management.
UX opportunity
Make filters, shopping information and the loyalty programme immediately accessible.
PERSONA 02 · 22 YEARS OLD

Luca

Anthropology student · vegetarian

He discovers iVegan through a friend and would like to shop with a brand aligned with his values, especially on mobile.

What she looks for
Fast shopping, home delivery and verifiable ethical information.
What holds him back
Switching between site, shop and app, hidden costs and a lengthy sign-up.
UX opportunity
Unify the journeys, simplify access and clarify totals and shipping.

Summary of the personas developed for the project. Ages, roles and scenarios come from the Discovery 02 material.

02 · AS-IS USER JOURNEY

Identifying where the journey breaks down

The as-is journeys reconstruct two shopping scenarios. Sonia arrives at the site looking for specific products; Luca starts from a recommendation and tries to use the app.

Although their goals differ, both encounter difficulties understanding the offering, navigating the experience and moving between cart, sign-up and payment.

CURRENT JOURNEY · SONIA

From online search to checkout

AS-IS
  1. 01 · SEARCH Discovery

    Ethics and Made in Italy are difficult to see in search.

  2. 02 · TRUST Verification

    Reviews and brand information are difficult to find.

  3. 03 · NAVIGATION Categories

    Unintuitive labels and gluten-/sugar-free filters are difficult to find.

  4. 04 · CART Costs and access

    Minimum spend is difficult to see and sign-up is complex.

  5. 05 · CHECKOUT Loyalty

    Bonuses and rewards are not immediately understandable.

CURRENT JOURNEY · LUCA

From recommendation to shopping in the app

AS-IS
  1. 01 · ACCESS Discovery

    The separate site and shop make it less clear where to start.

  2. 02 · APP Orientation

    Product presentation and categories are inconsistent.

  3. 03 · CARRELLO Total

    Total price and shipping are not immediately visible.

  4. 04 · SIGN-UP Breakdown

    The cart is emptied after sign-up and the address is difficult to enter.

  5. 05 · PAYMENT Confirmation

    Limited methods and order messages create uncertainty.

CROSS-CUTTING INSIGHT

The problem is not one screen, but the continuity of the journey.

Incomplete information, poorly visible cart rules and discontinuous steps between platforms increase the effort required before completing the purchase.

03 · OPPORTUNITIES AND NEW JOURNEYS

Turning barriers into redesign decisions

The pain points from the two journeys are connected to concrete interventions in navigation, interface, cart, login and trust content.

The new journeys represent proposed design scenarios: they describe how the redesigned experience could work, not results already verified through user testing.

AS-IS PAIN POINTS REDESIGN PROPOSAL

Fragmented navigation
Categories are not immediately clear and the sites are separate.

→

A unified and intuitive structure
Reordered categories, clear filters and consistent paths between site and app.

Trust to rebuild
Reviews and supply-chain information are difficult to find.

→

Accessible transparency
A dedicated hub for ethics, origin, certifications, reviews and recipes.

Unpredictable cart
Minimum spend, shipping and discounts appear too late.

→

Costs always visible
Updated total, explicit thresholds and clear voucher application.

Interrupted checkout
Lengthy sign-up, cart not preserved and unclear confirmations.

→

Continuous shopping journey
Social login, preserved cart, clear fields and order confirmation.

TWO PROPOSED NEW JOURNEYS

How the experience could change

SONIA

From ethical information to checkout

  1. 01 Discovery

    The brand makes ethics and Made in Italy visible from search onwards.

  2. 02 Trust

    Reviews, supply chain and certifications are easy to explore.

  3. 03 Products

    Categories and filters help users find GF/SF options.

  4. 04 Carrello

    Clear costs and thresholds; sign-up without losing items.

  5. 05 Purchase

    Loyalty vouchers managed and applied at checkout.

LUCA

From site to app without unnecessary steps

  1. 01 Access

    One site presents the brand and makes the app visible.

  2. 02 Exploration

    The blog, recipes and categories lead to relevant products.

  3. 03 Carrello

    Total, thresholds and shipping are immediately understandable.

  4. 04 Account

    Quick login and a cart preserved after sign-up.

  5. 05 Payment

    More methods, explicit confirmations and order status.

Summary of the new journey maps: the steps represent the designed experience, not a flow that has already been implemented or measured.

04 · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

A structure that makes choices easier

The new sitemap organises content around the main activities: finding products, understanding the brand identity and completing a purchase without losing information or progress.

The proposal unifies the web presence, connects the app to the site experience and makes categories, filters, trust features and account services more visible.
