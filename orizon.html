BRAND IDENTITY & SOCIAL MEDIA · ORIZON

A coherent identity, from brand to content

Orizon is a brand concept dedicated to responsible and regenerative tourism. The project starts by defining the brand values and translates them into a recognisable visual system designed to work from the logo through to digital applications.

The same identity is then adapted for social media by differentiating formats, hierarchy and the amount of information according to each channel and audience, without losing consistency across touchpoints.

01 · LOGO & BRAND IDENTITY

Translating responsible travel into a visual system

Orizon is based on the idea of promoting a more conscious, sustainable and regenerative way of travelling. The brand centres the balance between travellers and local communities, with experiences designed to create value for both people and places.

The visual identity therefore starts from ideas of connection, reciprocity, authenticity and respect. The aim was not only to create a recognisable mark, but a language capable of carrying these values across different contexts.

01 Vision

Design memorable journeys that respect the environment and value local communities.

02 Mission

Help people travel in a more conscious, sustainable and regenerative way.

03 Guiding principle

Quality, personalisation and fair exchange to leave a positive impact on the places visited.

BRAND CONCEPT

Hand and spiral as symbols of connection

The mark combines a hand and a solar spiral in one form. The spiral occupies the centre of the palm, while the fingers use warm, earthy tones. The visual reference recalls symbols from Native American and Asian cultures and aims to evoke vital energy, human connection and a conscious footprint.

The logotype retains a spontaneous brushstroke to communicate craft and authenticity; the payoff “Make a difference, one trip at a time” connects travel to the idea of change created through small actions.

VISUAL SYSTEM

An identity designed to remain consistent across touchpoints

01 Typography

Brushberry Sans One for the logotype, Amatic SC for the payoff, Barlow Condensed for headings and social content, and Neue Haas Grotesque Text Pro for reading text.

02 Palette

Natural, deep tones inspired by earth, wood, vegetation and light to express authenticity, sustainability and regeneration.

03 Icon system

Grid-based icons with a brushed stroke to maintain balance, clarity and continuity with the logotype.

ACCESSIBILITY & GUIDELINES

Protecting brand legibility and recognisability

01 Contrast

The brand colours were checked against WCAG criteria to maintain adequate readability in the main combinations.

02 Correct use

The logo is applied on a white background or a white brushed surface when placed over photographs.

03 What to avoid

Chromatically complex backgrounds or backgrounds without a contrast area, which reduce legibility and recognisability.

02 · SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT DESIGN

Adapting content to the audience and platform

Social communication maintains the brand’s visual system but avoids replicating the same content on every platform. Format, amount of text and hierarchy change according to how people use Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.

The aim is to build recognisability without compromising each channel’s role: more explicit information on Facebook, authentic and concise visuals on Instagram, and longer, more in-depth storytelling on YouTube.

01 Facebook

Clear messages and information available directly in the post, with more space for explanation.

02 Instagram

Authentic aesthetics, natural colours and few graphic elements, favouring imagery that feels less constructed and more immersive.

03 YouTube

Vlogs, personal stories and thematic content about culture, sustainability and responsible tourism.

OUTPUT BY CHANNEL

Same identity, different formats and goals

FACEBOOK Brand, events and services

Cover and profile image, 1200×630px brand-introduction post, and 1080×1350px vertical content for events and travel.

INSTAGRAM 4:5 vertical visuals

1080×1350px posts about the brand, travel and events, with immersive imagery and essential information.

YOUTUBE Channel and storytelling

Channel profile and cover, plus 1280×720px video previews for vlogs and in-depth content.

CROSS-CUTTING PRINCIPLE

Consistency does not mean uniformity.

The logo, palette and visual tone keep the brand recognisable; what changes is how the content is prioritised and presented, so that each platform can fulfil a specific function.
