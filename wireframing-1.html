01 · PRELIMINARY EXERCISE

Reconstructing before redesigning

In the first phase, I analysed PlantX and reconstructed the Home, PLP and PDP in desktop and mobile versions. The aim was to understand the existing structure, spacing, alignment and components, including its inconsistencies , not redesign them.

Reviewing Chrome DevTools revealed extensive use of Flexbox, inconsistent margins and gutters, uneven typography, competing CTAs and several contrast issues. I used 12-column desktop and 4-column mobile grids to organise the reconstruction.

02 · PAIN POINTS AND INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

A more predictable navigation structure

Evidence from the journeys and accessibility analysis was translated into concrete goals: unify site and shop, make search, wishlist, account and cart immediately recognisable, make costs and thresholds explicit, and give trust content more space.

The new structure distinguishes product access, ethics and sustainability content, and shopping services. The sitemap in the slides includes link types and dynamic behaviours; the version shown here is a summary rather than the complete structure.

IDENTIFIED PAIN POINT PROPOSAL
Fragmented architecture

Site/shop and categories are disconnected.

→
Unified access

Organised navigation and products that are easier to reach.

Information is difficult to see

Ethics, supply chain, origin and reviews are difficult to find.

→
Information hub

Sections dedicated to transparency, brand and certifications.

Unpredictable checkout

Thresholds, shipping costs and vouchers appear too late.

→
Earlier feedback

Total and conditions visible throughout the journey.

03 · MID-FIDELITY WIREFRAMES

From the layout system to desktop screens

The iVegan wireframes were developed with a Bootstrap grid of 12 columns, 15px margins and 30px gutters . Inter styles and a Minor Third (1.2) modular scale were defined for typography; greys and blue distinguish structure from actions.

The screens were selected for their role in the funnel: from the homepage and catalogue browsing, through the product page and cart, to the Ethics & Sustainability Hub.

01 · HOME

Orientation and trust

Top bar with language choice and app access; primary CTA and sections for ethics, categories, offers and reviews.

02 · PLP

Search and comparison

Breadcrumb, diet and sustainability filters, result count, informative cards and sorting.

03 · PDP

Information for decision-making

Availability, brand, allergens, reviews, impact and a CTA aligned with product status.

04 · CART

Transparency before checkout

Pop-up and summary with total, minimum threshold, shipping, discounts and checkout.

05 · ETHICS HUB

Access to brand values

Content about ethics, sustainability, supply chain, certifications and a link to the shop.

04 · DESKTOP WIREFLOW

Checking continuity between screens

The Pt. 1 wireflow takes the user from accessing products to confirming the order. It represents login/sign-up, address entry and payment selection. The focus is experience continuity, not testing results on an already developed product.

  1. 01 Home / menu

    Vegan products → Vegan meat

  2. 02 PLP / PDP

    Product selection and add to cart.

  3. 03 Cart pop-up

    Total, thresholds, vouchers and access to checkout.

  4. 04 Login / sign-up

    Social or email access; cart preserved.

  5. 05 Address / payment

    Delivery details, payment method and confirmation.
